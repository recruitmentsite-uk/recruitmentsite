# Competitive positioning — Recruitment Site

## UK market landscape

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness | Our response |
|------------|----------|----------|--------------|
| **Reed.co.uk** | Brand, CV database, huge inventory | Expensive featured posts, generic | Niche vertical depth + AI matching |
| **Indeed** | Traffic, PPC model | Race to bottom on quality, ghost jobs | Verified employers only, salary required |
| **Hays / Michael Page** | Enterprise relationships | 15–25% fees, human-heavy | Flat £249/mo unlimited for SMEs |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional network | £££ for SMEs, noisy | Better SME pricing, faster apply |
| **Totaljobs / CV-Library** | Legacy CV stock | Dated UX, declining | Modern UX, mobile-first apply |
| **Otta / Welcome to the Jungle** | Tech startup brand | Limited verticals | Copy playbook for Healthcare/Trades |

## Positioning statement

> **Recruitment Site** is the flat-fee hiring platform for UK SMEs — unlimited jobs, AI-matched candidates, no agency commission.

## Differentiators (defensible)

1. **Vertical-first SEO** — own "NHS nurse jobs Manchester" not "jobs UK"
2. **AI match scores visible to employers** — saves screening time (measurable ROI)
3. **Salary transparency required** — builds candidate trust, Google Jobs ranking boost
4. **Automation = lower price** — pass savings vs Reed/Hays to employers
5. **Compliance built-in** — GDPR retention, equality monitoring exports

## Wedge verticals (pick ONE to start)

### 1. Healthcare (recommended)
- Chronic staff shortage (NHS + private care)
- Employers: care homes, NHS trusts, locum agencies
- High willingness to pay (£249–499/mo is cheap vs agency fees)
- Compliance hooks: DBS, NMC pin, right to work

### 2. Trades & construction
- Electricians, plumbers, site managers
- CIS/payment terms awareness in job templates
- Competitors: Checkatrade jobs, Randstad CPE

### 3. Tech (crowded but proven)
- Otta proved startup hiring works
- Harder SEO; only if you have network

## Moat over time

- **Candidate graph** — profiles + application history = better matching
- **Employer retention** — careers page subdomain, ATS integration lock-in
- **SEO corpus** — thousands of `/jobs/{role}-{city}` pages
- **Brand in one vertical** — "the place for healthcare jobs"

## What "compete with majors" actually means

You won't out-spend Reed on TV ads. You **can**:

- Win a vertical where Reed is a generalist
- Offer 10× better unit economics to SMEs
- Rank on long-tail Google searches within 6 months
- Automate what agencies charge humans for (screening, shortlisting)

**Realistic outcome:** £500k–£2m ARR automated platform in 18–24 months, not a £100m Reed killer — but very profitable for a small team.
